# Liturgy

> *Cultivating a sincere era of electronics and software.*

**Liturgy** is an independent engineering bureau working across electronics, embedded systems and software. We design and build systems that keep control with the people who use them: tools that treat individuals as people, not as consumers.

Our work sits where precise timing, clean signals and decentralized control meet. We aim to be a steady, dependable point of contact for clients and collaborators who value clear engineering and plain dealing.

## What We Do

We take projects from first sketch to working system, whether that is a single circuit board or a complete platform.

- **Custom software and web applications**
- **IoT and embedded systems:** firmware, networking and hardware
- **PCB and hardware design**
- **UI/UX and product management**
- **Multimedia solutions**
- **Technical consulting**

Our experience spans software architecture, broadcast engineering, and CNC and CAM manufacturing, and we work comfortably across those boundaries.

## Principles of Operation

Our approach is consistent, though not dogmatic.

- **Autonomy.** Users should control their own tools. We build systems that do not depend on third-party services for core functionality, and we say plainly where they cannot.
- **Consonance.** Clarity and equilibrium in design. Every part has a reason to be there, and the parts agree with one another.
- **The Cosmic Perspective.** We keep a long view and keep our attention on the work, rather than spending energy on needless friction.

## Technical Standards

We hold ourselves to high standards for signal quality and timekeeping, and our systems are time-locked to UTC. We cooperate with wider networks and services where they serve the user, and we do not rely on them to make a system work.

## Current Work

**[Observe Pulsar](https://github.com/ChrisKarayann/observe-pulsar-showcase)** is a private, encrypted communication system for physical devices, built in Rust on ESP32 hardware and WireGuard, with no cloud broker and no subscription. It is in an early, bench-proven stage.

## Working With Us

We take on projects where there is a clear purpose and a good fit, and we prefer collaborations built on openness and straightforward communication. If you are considering working with us, write with a short description of what you would like to build. Where the fit is not right, we will say so politely and plainly.
